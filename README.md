# Amazon India Sales & Profit Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Amazon India Sales & Profit Dashboard is an Excel-based business intelligence project designed to analyze sales, profitability, orders, products, payment methods, fulfillment methods, and geographic performance.

The dashboard provides a simple management-level view of business performance and highlights key areas that require attention.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify high-performing categories and products
- Analyze order status and revenue loss
- Evaluate payment method performance
- Evaluate fulfillment method performance
- Analyze state-wise sales, profit, and orders
- Create a management-friendly dashboard using Excel

- Electronics & Mobiles is the strongest business category.
- UPI is the leading payment method based on sales and profit.

## Overall Conclusion

Overall, the Amazon India business demonstrates strong sales and profitability, with Electronics & Mobiles serving as the primary business-driving category. UPI is the leading payment method and Amazon FBA is the dominant fulfillment channel. Maharashtra, Karnataka and Delhi are important geographic markets based on sales and order activity.

Although most orders are successfully delivered, returns and cancellations remain areas for improvement. Management should focus on strengthening high-performing categories and markets while reducing returns and cancellations and maintaining healthy profitability.
